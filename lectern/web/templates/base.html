Questions

What lectures work best?
English lectures up to two hours, with slides, a whiteboard, a blackboard or a tablet. Talking-head stretches are fine, they just add nothing to read.
Can it read handwriting?
Yes. Each frame is read together with what the lecturer was saying at that moment, which settles most messy handwriting. Anything it can't read is marked, not guessed.
How do I know the notes are right?
Every note, card and quiz answer links to the moment in the lecture it came from, so you can check it in one click.
Why do I need an access code?
Each lecture spends transcription and AI credits, so uploads are limited to people with a code. The sample courses are free to browse.
